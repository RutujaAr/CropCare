About CropCare

A simple educational portal for learning about crop diseases and general management practices.

Our Purpose

Making crop disease information easier to explore.

CropCare is a web-based crop disease information and management portal created for agricultural learning and awareness.

The application allows users to browse selected crops, search for diseases, and explore information about symptoms, favorable conditions, management and prevention.

The project demonstrates how a simple web application can be connected to a modern automated CI/CD workflow using GitHub, GitHub Actions and Vercel.

Educational Information

CropCare is intended for educational and general agricultural awareness purposes. It does not provide automated plant disease diagnosis or replace professional agricultural advice.
